14 · Load-bearing constraints

Do not design around these.

1

Integrations are surfaces, never dependencies (ADR-6). No feature may require a specific third-party product. Core, CLI, MCP, and epitod work with zero integrations beyond the user's chosen identity target.

2

The manifest is publishable, always. Secrets live in .epito/ or the platform's vault, never in agent.toml. Break this once and the future marketplace is dead before it exists.

3

Core never grows a platform branch. A misfit integration means the seam is wrong. Fix the interface and record the ADR.

4

Local-first survives the hosted plane. Hosted mode is a provider swap. The CLI and epitod keep working with no account on our servers, permanently.

The owner enforces these four at every merge. Everything else in this deck is implementation and may change by ordinary ADR.

Closing slide. These constraints are the epito equivalent of Ursa's "load-bearing constraints" section.
