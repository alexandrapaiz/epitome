7 · The traps, attractive and wrong

Three doors the map says not to walk through.

T1

Cross-org A2A and enterprise agent marketplaces. A2A runs in production across 150 orgs and the shelves multiply — but every shelf has a bar we fail by construction, the buyers are procurement-cycle enterprises, and A2A solves messaging between agents, not principal-hood on platforms. Non-trap version: an A2A agent card from epitod someday, as interop.

T2

Agentic payments (ACP, AP2 mandates, x402). The loudest space in the room and the wrong first proof for an identity product: one payment incident is existential. Read-only reconcile-and-flag stays allowed; money movement does not.

T3

The enterprise "digital employee" wave. Gartner expects over 40% of those projects dead by 2027. Our buyer stays the builder with a roster, not the enterprise buying headcount replacement.

Competitive tripwire, checked this run: Anthropic Managed Agents still holds the owner's keys — no per-agent platform identity found. The trigger has not fired.
