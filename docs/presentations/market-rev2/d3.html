Decision 3: three markets we recommend refusing.

yes / no, each

Enterprise agent app-stores

Every shelf (Microsoft, Google, Salesforce) requires partner programs and verified-business bars we fail today, to reach buyers with year-long purchasing cycles.

Agent payments

The loudest protocol race of the year, but one payment mistake would be fatal to a young identity product. Agents read financial data; they do not move money. Not before 2028.

Selling "digital employees" to big companies

Gartner projects over 40% of those projects canceled by end of 2027 and estimates only about 130 of the thousands of vendors are real. Our buyer stays the individual builder running a roster of agents.
