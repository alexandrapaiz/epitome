4 · Watch and skip, the load-bearing rows

The giants validate the thesis; the middlemen are the anti-model.

Microsoft: third-party agents can now federate into a customer's Entra tenant (Auth SDK sidecar, workload identity). The future enterprise story is "epito agents, governed in your Entra" — a federation path, not a 2026 build. Their per-user pricing means their buyer is not ours.

AWS + Google: AgentCore workload identity (own ARN per agent) and per-agent SPIFFE IDs validate the identity-primitive thesis from the two biggest clouds. Watch the primitives, skip the platforms. An A2A agent card from epitod someday is cheap interop, not strategy.

Linear: still the cleanest primitive (agents as unbilled, assignable app users). Owner-rejected as #2 under ADR-6; stays on the board and returns only on her word.

Skip: Zapier / Make. The vendor names its own model: 9,000+ apps behind "one set of managed credentials" in Zapier's vault. A master key held by a middleman is precisely what our primitive replaces. No per-agent principal in their model.

Skip: Salesforce AgentExchange. Security review + partner program + enterprise motion; even their $50M builders fund concedes the bar. Slack covers the ecosystem's useful surface at 1% of the cost.

Clerk: ally, not channel — CIMD (an HTTPS URL as OAuth client_id) could give every epito agent a stable OAuth client identity. Mechanism to fold in later.
