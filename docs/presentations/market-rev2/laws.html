1 · Ranking laws, do not route around these

Four laws decide every tier.

1

Surface, never dependency (ADR-6). Core, epito, the MCP server, and epitod work with zero integrations installed.

2

Self-serve mint or no build slot. Partner approvals, waitlists, revenue bars, and sales calls can be watched but cannot hold a slot. GitHub and Slack qualify; Vercel's marketplace and Supabase's catalog do not, yet.

3

The agent appears as itself or the row is mislabeled. Acting under the owner's token or a shared installation is delegation, not identity, and ranks accordingly.

4

Distribution is a bonus on a working surface, never the reason it exists. A listing bar may gate the storefront; it must never gate the product working.
