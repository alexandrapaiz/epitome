One file describes the agent.

One command gives it a real identity, a schedule, and a home on your own machine.

The identity is yours to limit and yours to cancel.

This is ADR-1 and ADR-2 (docs/decisions.md) in plain words: the manifest (agent.toml) mints identity + trigger + runtime. "Yours to limit and yours to cancel" translates scoped and revocable. "On your own machine" is the phase-2 daemon (docs/architecture.md, epitod): a persistent container per agent, woken on a schedule or an event, no human session needed. Do not say manifest, principal, container, or cron on the slide.
